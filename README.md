# Day 02 — E-commerce Customer RFM Analysis:

## Problem statement
Who are our most valuable customers and which ones are at risk of churning?

## Dataset
- Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository — Online Retail
- 541,909 transactions · UK online retailer · 2010–2011

## What is RFM?
RFM segments customers by Recency (how recently they bought),
Frequency (how often) and Monetary value (how much they spend).

## Key findings
- Champions drive the majority of revenue despite being a small % of customers
- A large At Risk segment needs immediate retention campaigns
- UK dominates revenue — international markets are a growth opportunity
- Top 10 customers account for a disproportionate share of total spend
